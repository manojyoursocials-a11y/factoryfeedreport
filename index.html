Engagement Ideas
Behind-the-scenes stories. Post a "we're planning to shoot" or live update story — e.g. "Shooting today in Sudama Nagar, Chickpet" — to keep followers tuned in to what's coming.
Polls in stories. Add polls to gather quick feedback from followers — which supplier videos they want next, which category to cover, etc.
Talk about the app, not just the reel. Every so often, point viewers back to the app instead of just the content — e.g. "Want to check the full CMAI Fair videos? Watch on the Factoryfeed App."
Community touchpoints. Use WhatsApp & Instagram Communities to drop quick updates, respond to questions, and keep suppliers/buyers engaged between posts.
